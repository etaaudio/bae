Crossfeed mixes a little of each channel into the other, low-passed and slightly delayed, so headphones sound less split down the middle. It is for headphones only.

Clip guard keeps padded bits and B-Mode from clipping, in one of two ways. Intelligent lets the full gain through and holds individual peaks down as they come, like a mastering limiter: the most loudness, at the cost of some squashing when it works hard. Gain limit does no processing at all: it simply never adds more gain than there is room for under 0 dB, which is however far the video's own volume is turned down. At full volume that is no room, so nothing is added; lower the video's volume slider to make room.

How BAE gets at the sound for everything in this section. Tap asks the browser to route it, which Safari doesn't do for YouTube or Spotify. Stream capture asks for a live copy instead. Rebuild reads YouTube's audio as it downloads, decodes it and plays it itself in step with the picture; it is the one to try in Safari. Rebuild reads AAC audio, so on that path BAE asks YouTube for AAC whatever the codec picker says. Reload the tab after changing this.

B-Mode lifts the low bass with a gentle shelf, reaching +12 dB at the chosen frequency. 10 Hz is the mildest, 40 Hz the heaviest. The overall level drops 12 dB to make room for it, so turn your amp up.
