# BAE privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

BAE (B-audio Enhancer) is a browser extension published by ETA Audio LLC.

## What BAE collects

Nothing. BAE does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal information or
browsing data.

## What BAE stores

Your own settings (codec choice, Purist and normalization options, playback speed,
pitch correction, crossfeed, B-Mode, clip guard, output device and display options) are saved in your
browser's local extension storage so they persist
between sessions. They stay on your device. Removing the extension removes them.

## Network requests

BAE makes no network requests. It has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party
code.

## Sites it runs on

BAE runs only on the few media sites named in its permissions, which your browser shows
before you install it. On those pages it reads which audio and video formats the player
uses and adjusts its volume, speed, audio processing and output device. It does not read
any other page content.

## Microphone prompt

If you use "Show my output devices", your browser asks for microphone access on that
site, because browsers only list audio outputs for sites allowed to use the microphone.
BAE switches the microphone off again immediately and never records or listens.
