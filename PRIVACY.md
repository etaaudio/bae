# BAE privacy policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

BAE (B-audio Enhancer) is a browser extension published by ETA Audio LLC.

## What BAE collects

Nothing. BAE does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal information or
browsing data.

## What BAE stores

Your own settings (codec choice, Purist and normalization options, playback speed,
pitch correction, crossfeed, B-Mode, clip guard, output device and display options) are
saved in your browser's local extension storage so they persist between sessions. They
stay on your device. Removing the extension removes them.

## Network requests

BAE sends nothing anywhere. It has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code.

To show the sample rate and bit depth of what is playing, BAE may request the first part
of the audio file or stream playlist the page is already playing, from the same address
the page uses. Nothing about you is added to that request, and what comes back is only
read for its format and then discarded.

## Sites it runs on

BAE runs on every website, so its settings work in any tab that plays audio or video. On
each page it reads which audio and video formats the player asks for and uses, and
adjusts its volume, speed, audio processing and output device. It does not read any
other page content.

## Microphone prompt

If you use "Show my output devices", your browser asks for microphone access on that
site, because browsers only list audio outputs for sites allowed to use the microphone.
BAE switches the microphone off again immediately and never records or listens.
