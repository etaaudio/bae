# BAE privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

BAE (B-audio Enhancer) is a browser extension published by ETA Audio LLC.

## What BAE collects

Nothing. BAE does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal information or
browsing data.

## What BAE stores

Your own settings (codec choice, playback speed, pitch correction, output device and
display options) are saved in your browser's local extension storage so they persist
between sessions. They stay on your device. Removing the extension removes them.

## Network requests

BAE makes no network requests. It has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party
code.

## Sites it runs on

BAE runs only on youtube.com, youtube-nocookie.com and open.spotify.com. On those pages
it reads which audio and video formats the media player uses and adjusts the player's
volume, playback speed and audio output device. It does not read any other page content.

## Microphone prompt

If you use "Show my output devices", your browser asks for microphone access on that
site, because browsers only list audio outputs for sites allowed to use the microphone.
BAE switches the microphone off again immediately and never records or listens.
